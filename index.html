Due diligence

What buyers should verify

Anyone considering property should complete proper due diligence before paying money or signing an agreement. Important checks include:

  • Property ownership and title documents
  • Relevant authority approvals
  • Plot or property location
  • Development status
  • Transfer requirements
  • Outstanding dues
  • Applicable taxes and charges
  • Seller identity and authority to sell
  • Current market value
  • Official project information

Using independent legal and property professionals can also be helpful when the transaction involves significant money.
